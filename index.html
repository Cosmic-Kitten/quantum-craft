Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about building with Quantum Studios.

What does "Open Source First" mean for my website? +
It means you own 100% of the code. We build your site in a clean, transparent repository structure without proprietary page-builder lock-ins, giving you complete freedom to host, fork, or modify your application whenever you choose.
How long does a custom build typically take? +
Landing pages are usually delivered within 3 to 5 business days. Full multi-page business sites take around 1 to 2 weeks, depending on asset availability and specific design requirements.
Where will my website be hosted? +
We deploy websites seamlessly onto high-performance cloud providers like Vercel or Netlify, ensuring blistering load speeds, free SSL certificates, and global edge caching out of the box.
How do I receive project inquiry notifications? +
We integrate secure direct notification handlers (such as Web3Forms) directly into your contact form so emails hit your inbox instantly upon submission.
